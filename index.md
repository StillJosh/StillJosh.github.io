I am a PhD student at the [AIML chair at LMU Munich](https://www.ifi.lmu.de/kiml/en/), supervised by Eyke Hüllermeier and funded by the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML). Before my PhD, I studied mathematics at TU Munich.

My research is on machine learning for partial differential equations and dynamical systems. Current interests:

- Neural operators
- Learning dynamics from data
- Uncertainty quantification for PDE surrogates
